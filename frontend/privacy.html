Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

1. Information We Collect

CampusConnect may collect the following information when you use our platform:

  • Name, email address and account information.
  • Event registration and participation records.
  • Attendance and certificate information.
  • Messages and details submitted through the contact form.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Allow students to discover and register for events.
  • Help organizers manage events and registrations.
  • Track attendance and manage certificates.
  • Respond to queries and maintain platform security.

3. Information Sharing

Your information may be accessible to authorized organizers and administrators when necessary for event management, registration verification, attendance tracking and certificate processing.

We do not claim to sell your personal information.

4. Account Security

We take reasonable steps to protect account information and prevent unauthorized access. Users should keep their login credentials confidential and report suspicious account activity.

5. Data Accuracy

Users are responsible for providing accurate information during registration and keeping their account details up to date.

6. Third-Party Services

CampusConnect may rely on third-party services for database hosting, authentication or other technical operations. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

7. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when platform features or information-handling practices change. Updated information will be published on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact Page.
